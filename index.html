Turnkey Diagnostic Suite

SJ Meditek Standalone PACS

A high-speed, self-contained local PACS server designed specifically for standalone diagnostic scan centres, ultrasound clinics, and single-modality suites. Zero heavy database overhead and instant setup.

  • Single-Executable Architecture: Turnkey local server with 1-click startup, requiring zero complex SQL server management.
  • Direct Modality Connection: Plug-and-play DICOM interfacing with DR, CR, CT, MRI, Ultrasound, and C-Arm machines.
  • Instant Local Review: Sub-second study search and full diagnostic viewer toolset directly on your reporting PC.
  • Built-In DVD/USB Media Burning: 1-click disc creation with bundled offline HTML5 viewer — patients and doctors can view studies anywhere without installing software.
  • Zero Recurring Cloud Bandwidth Costs: Fully operable over local hospital LAN without mandatory internet connections.

Hospital & Network Scale

SJ Meditek Enterprise Main PACS

High-concurrency centralized imaging repository for multi-speciality hospitals, imaging chains, and teleradiology networks. Complete HIS/RIS integration with automated routing and multi-department security.

  • High-Availability DICOM Server: Centralized C-STORE, C-FIND, C-MOVE, C-ECHO, and full RESTful DICOMweb (WADO-RS / QIDO-RS / STOW-RS).
  • Modality Worklist (MWL) & HIS/RIS: Automated bidirectional synchronization with hospital admission and billing to eliminate manual entry errors.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Granular permissions for Super Admin, Radiologists, Clinical Consultants, Technicians, and Referring Physicians.
  • Clinical Reporting & Prior Archiving: Attach finalized PDF/JPEG reports directly to studies with instant historical comparison.

Specialized Cardiology Solution

Dedicated Cath Lab & Angiography Viewer

High-framerate dynamic fluoroscopy demands exceptional temporal resolution and zero stutter. SJ Meditek's Cath Lab viewer is purpose-built for interventional cardiologists and catheterization suites.

  • Smooth Cine Loop Playback: High frame-rate playback (15, 30, and 60 FPS) with instant forward/backward frame scrubbing.
  • Quantitative Coronary Analysis (QCA): Automated and manual stenosis percentage calculations, reference vessel diameter, and calibration.
  • Digital Subtraction Angiography (DSA): Real-time mask subtraction, roadmapping, and peak opacification display.
  • Multi-Modality Compatibility: Rigorously tested with Philips Azurion, GE Innova / Optima IGS 320, and Siemens Artis systems.
