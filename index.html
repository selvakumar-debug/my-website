How a breakdown call works

A clear path from your call to a working machine.

  1. You call or messageTell us the equipment, the fault and what the display shows.
  2. We diagnoseRemote guidance where possible, so simple faults are cleared quickly.
  3. Engineer on siteIf it needs hands-on work, an engineer visits, day or night.
  4. Repair and testThe fault is fixed and the equipment is checked before handover.
  5. Follow-upWe note the cause and advise how to prevent a repeat.
